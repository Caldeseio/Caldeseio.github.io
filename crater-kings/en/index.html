Crater Kings

Betting races on faraway planets · Android

Coming soon to Google Play

Two cars start back to back on a tiny planet. Each one drives half the planet in the opposite direction towards the same finish line, and near the end your rival shows up head-on. Speed up, brake, tilt the car in the air to land on its wheels and save the afterburner for the right moment: if the roof hits hard, the car breaks and the race is lost.

In the hangar, challengers arrive one at a time with an offer. Raise or lower the stake, turn them down, or bet the whole cars: the winner keeps the other one's parts. With your winnings you repair and upgrade your car, build your reputation and travel to harder planets, all the way to the moon where the King defends his crown.

What's inside

  • 3 universes, 15 planets and 96 rivals, each with a character of their own.
  • 3 engines, 3 afterburners, 9 chassis and 7 sets of tires: parts wear out and can be repaired.
  • Reputation, a racing certificate and crowns you can come back to defend.
  • Record mode with a top-10 table.
  • Play with the touch screen (buttons you can move and resize), a keyboard or a controller.
  • Bets only use in-game credits: no real money and no in-app purchases.
  • Ads only if you want them: in the garage you can watch up to 3 a day for credits.
  • Plays offline. In English and Spanish.
